# Georgian word-ladder graph: findings

Every number here comes from `reports/stats.json`, regenerated by `python -m ladder.analyze`.
Interactive version: `web/index.html` (path finder plus these charts).

## 1. The word list

Six open sources were merged and normalised to the 33-letter modern Mkhedruli alphabet
(Mtavruli capitals folded, anything with digits, Latin or archaic letters dropped):

| Source | Raw size | What it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| GeoWordsDatabase (Bumbeishvili, MIT) | 310k words, 106 sources | literature, textbooks, dictionaries, with counts; also a 2012 voter list (names) |
| GeoWords web scrape (Jvaridze) | 4.84M strings + counts | frequency signal; full of typos |
| akalongman/geo-words (MIT) | 193k words | National Parliamentary Library dictionary + ebooks |
| Hunspell `ka_GE` (MIT / CC-BY) | 143k entries | spell-checker word list |
| Crúbadán (Scannell, CC-BY 4.0) | top 50k words | web-crawl frequencies |
| English Wiktionary via kaikki.org (CC-BY-SA) | 27k entries | lemmas, part of speech, English glosses, declension tables |

Rejected: the OpenSubtitles frequency list for Georgian (`hermitdave/FrequencyWords ka`) is
Cyrillic subtitles decoded with the wrong code page (`ჟოაჟთბჲ`, `ოპთწრვლთრვ`), so it is useless.

Pitfall worth knowing: the GeoWords "dictionary" sources are every word printed in the
dictionary's text, definitions included. They contain ვაშლს and ვაშლის as well as ვაშლი, so they
prove a word exists but not that it is a headword.

**Tiers** (1,805,688 distinct strings in total):

| Tier | Words | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| headword | 20,522 | Wiktionary lemma (17.9k, with English gloss), or a nominative whose case forms (‑ს, ‑მა, ‑ში, ‑ზე, ‑თან) are attested (2.6k) |
| form | 166,516 | any other form attested in ≥ 3 independent sources (≥ 4 letters unless Wiktionary lists it) |
| proper | 8,128 | voter-list first names and surnames, Wiktionary proper nouns |
| rare | 1,610,522 | everything weaker: typos, one-off forms, abbreviations (no vowel), `ეეე`-style interjections |

89% of the distinct strings in the raw sources are noise. The cross-source vote does most of the cleaning.

## 2. The question: ვაშლი → მსხალი

**4 steps in both modes, and 4 is the minimum possible**: the Levenshtein distance between the two
words is 4, so no ladder can be shorter, and this one wastes no moves.

Dictionary words only (2 shortest ladders):

| step | ladder 1 | ladder 2 | edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| start | ვაშლი apple | ვაშლი apple | |
| 1 | ვალი debt | ვალი debt | −შ |
| 2 | სალი steep | ხალი birthmark, mole | ვ→ს / ვ→ხ |
| 3 | სხალი dialectal "pear" | სხალი dialectal "pear" | +ხ / +ს |
| 4 | მსხალი pear | მსხალი pear | +მ |

With inflected forms allowed there are 4 shortest ladders. The extra two go through მხალი, an old
word for greens or vegetables, which reached only the "form" tier because just one of its case forms is attested.

ვალი is the gateway: with 31 neighbours it is one of the biggest hubs among dictionary words.

There is one giant component and then dust: the runner-up is 150 to 450 times smaller. Inside the
giant, high assortativity means hubs link to hubs, so the dense core is a block of short words.

## 4. Findings

**Length decides everything.** Share of words in the main component, by length:

| letters | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dictionary words | 100% | 98.5% | 91.7% | 63% | 23% | 5.2% | 1.0% | 0% | 0% |
| all forms | 100% | 99.7% | 98.1% | 94.7% | 82% | 61% | 38% | 21% | ≤ 10% |

Georgian headwords average 8.4 letters (median 8). The English word-ladder sweet spot is 4–5 letters,
and Georgian vocabulary mostly lives above that, which is why the strict graph is sparse.

**Two different alphabets of moves.** Among dictionary words the commonest swaps are vowels:
ა↔ი (285), ა↔ო, ი↔ო, ა↔ე, ე↔ი. The liquids ლ↔რ follow. Edits land on the first letter 41% of the time
and on the last only 10%: rhyme families like ბარი / კარი / ხარი / ქარი. Once inflected forms are allowed,
the top swaps become grammar: ი↔ს (nominative ვაშლი ↔ dative ვაშლს), ა↔ი, თ↔ს. Last-letter edits
jump to 36%, and insertions/deletions (mostly ს, ა, ი) rise from 27% to 44% of all edges.

**Hubs are the CVC-ი template.** The most connected dictionary words are ბარი "valley" (37),
კარი "door" (34), ხარი "ox" (32), კერი, შარი, ვალი "debt" (31), ბალი "cherry" (30), ქარი "wind",
ბოლი "smoke", ხალი "mole". The top words by betweenness are almost the same set (არი, ბარი, ალი, სალი, ბალი, კარი).

**The longest ladders are fragile corridors.** The diameter of the dictionary graph is exactly 28:
მისეული "his/hers" → … → შეცოდება "sin". The ladder runs ‑ული adjectives → მოდელი → სველი → ბალა →
ბამბა → სამბა → სამება → აგება → აშენებს → შენება → შეცოდება. Most of its middle words are articulation
points. The top keystones are სამბა "samba" (removing it cuts 41 words off the main component),
სამება "trinity" (39), არეული "chaotic" (39) and ნება "will" (31). With forms allowed the diameter is
at least 47: ნორმებზე → … → მოუახლოვდება, mostly a walk through ‑ებზე plurals.

**Very common words can be hermits.** ძალიან "very", მაინც, შეიძლება, უკვე, მხოლოდ, ყველაფერი and
პირველი are frequent dictionary words with zero neighbours. They can never appear in a ladder.

## 5. What this means for the game

1. **Use the hybrid rule**: start and target words come from dictionary headwords, and any attested form is a legal step.
   The strict graph alone leaves 85% of headwords unreachable.
2. **Keep puzzle endpoints at 3–6 letters.** Among dictionary words, only 5.6% of 4-letter words are isolated,
   against 47% of 6-letter words and 77.5% of 8-letter words.
3. **Grade difficulty with two numbers**: ladder length and number of shortest ladders. Six steps with ×1 is much
   harder than six steps with ×35. `data/game/puzzles.tsv` stores both for 480 generated puzzles.
4. **Hand-check the keystones.** 918 dictionary words hold the main component together. One wrong or
   missing entry there (for example სამბა) silently breaks or creates many ladders.
5. **Show the gloss or lemma at each step.** Players will doubt words like სხალი or ვაშტი. Wiktionary glosses
   cover 17.9k headwords, and "form of …" links cover 54.8k of the 166.5k forms.
6. Known gaps: verbs are under-represented as headwords (Georgian has no infinitive, and Wiktionary lists
   about 1k verbs), the paradigm rule cannot tell foreign surnames from nouns, and archaic words like მხალი
   stay in the form tier. A licensed lemma list such as the Explanatory Dictionary of the Georgian Language
   would fix all three.
