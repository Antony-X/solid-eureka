    Hubs and keystones (dictionary words)

    Hubs have the most neighbours. Keystones are bottlenecks: removing one cuts that many words off from the main component.

    The longest ladders

    Diameter of the largest component: the two words that are furthest apart, with the forced route between them.

    Common words with no neighbours at all

    Frequent dictionary words that no single edit turns into another word. They can never appear in a ladder, so leave them out of puzzles.
